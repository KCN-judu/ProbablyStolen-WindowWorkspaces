# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Leaving the store (back to the main menu, say) no longer keeps it in memory. The mod held on to the store's windows after leaving it, which kept every item and inventory of that store alive and made later loads in the same session slower.

## 1.0.0 (pre-release)

First public release.

- Dragged windows snap to the edges of other windows and of the screen, and away from any edge to the inventory grid. A faint grid and guide lines show while dragging. Hold Alt to drag freely.
- Workspaces: named sets of open containers, like virtual desktops. Leaving a workspace remembers its windows, their positions and tab selections; entering one reopens them where they were.
- A workspace manager, built like the game's storage-preset manager, to switch, create, rename and delete workspaces and to take containers out of one.
- Two keys cover everything: the next workspace (Shift for the previous one) and the manager.
- In-game text in every language the game supports.
